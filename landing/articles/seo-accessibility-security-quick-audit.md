# SEO, Accessibility, and Security: One Pass, Not Three

Auditing a webpage often means jumping between Lighthouse, axe, security headers tools, and manual inspection. PageLens combines those checks into one Chrome toolbar action so you can review SEO, accessibility, and security in seconds.

## What to look for first

- **SEO:** title and description presence and length, heading structure, image alt text, canonical and robots meta tags.
- **Accessibility:** missing alt text, empty buttons, missing form labels, heading hierarchy, skip links, lang attribute.
- **Security:** HTTPS usage, mixed content, insecure forms, and visible security indicators.

Each of those categories benefits from being reviewed together instead of in isolation because they often overlap. A page with poor SEO structure frequently also has weak accessibility semantics.

## Speed over depth

Deep audits still matter. But most day-to-day review cycles need speed more than exhaustive coverage. PageLens is built for fast triage: open the page, click the icon, scan the tabs, export if needed.

## Exporting and sharing

When you find issues, you usually need to share them. JSON export supports automation and archiving. CSV export works in spreadsheets. PDF export supports client or stakeholder review.

## Bottom line

Review pages early and often. Use one-click auditing as a baseline check, not a replacement for deeper tools when needed.
