# PageLens — Landing Page Copy

## Headline

**PageLens — See Any Website's Health in One Click**

## Subhead

A free Chrome extension that audits any webpage for performance, SEO, accessibility, security, and technology stack — with a single click and a clear health score.

## Benefit Bullets

- **Instant Website Health Score** — Get a 0–100 score plus actionable issues across 7 categories: performance, SEO, accessibility, security, technology detection, links, and page overview. No setup, no config — just click.

- **Spot What Matters** — Detect 28 technologies (React, Vue, WordPress, Shopify, Stripe, HubSpot, and more), find broken SEO, catch accessibility gaps, and flag security risks — all from a clean, tabbed popup.

- **Export & Share** — Download a full JSON report of any page's analysis. Perfect for audits, client handoffs, or tracking improvements over time.
