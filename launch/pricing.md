# PageLens — Pricing Strategy

## Model: Freemium (Free + Pro Tier)

### Free Tier — PageLens

- Full page analysis (all 7 categories)
- Health score (0–100)
- Technology detection (28 signatures)
- JSON export (single page)
- Unlimited page audits

**Price: $0**

### Pro Tier — PageLens Pro

Everything in Free, plus:

- **Batch Export** — Export analysis for multiple pages at once (CSV + JSON)
- **Historical Tracking** — Save and compare page health scores over time
- **Custom Signatures** — Add your own technology detection rules
- **Priority Support** — Direct email support
- **White-label Reports** — Branded PDF reports for client delivery
- **API Access** — REST API for automated page analysis (100 calls/month)

**Price: $9/month or $79/year** (38% savings on annual)

## Rationale

- The free tier is fully functional as a single-page auditor — enough for casual users and developers doing spot checks.
- The Pro tier targets professionals (SEO consultants, agency developers, accessibility auditors) who need batch operations, historical data, and client-ready reports.
- $9/month is competitive with similar dev-tool extensions and low enough for impulse upgrades.
- Annual pricing incentivizes commitment and improves LTV.
