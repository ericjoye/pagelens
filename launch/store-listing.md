## Long Description

PageLens is a lightweight Chrome extension that gives you a comprehensive one-page audit of any website — instantly.

Click the PageLens toolbar icon on any page and get:

**Overview** — Overall health score (0–100), quick stats grid, and top issues at a glance.

**Performance** — Page weight, request count, resource breakdown, and timing metrics.

**SEO** — Title/description validation, heading structure audit, image alt text checks, canonical and robots meta analysis.

**Accessibility** — Missing alt text, missing form labels, heading hierarchy issues, buttons without text, skip links, and lang attribute checks.

**Technology** — Detects 28 technologies including React, Vue, Angular, jQuery, WordPress, Shopify, Stripe, HubSpot, Sentry, and more.

**Security** — HTTPS check, mixed content detection, insecure forms, and security headers (CSP, X-Frame-Options).

**Links** — Total, internal, external, nofollow, and new-tab link counts with full listing.

**Export** — Download the full analysis as a JSON report for audits, client handoffs, or tracking improvements.

PageLens runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no tracking, no external servers. Pure vanilla JavaScript with zero dependencies. Manifest V3 compatible.

Perfect for developers, designers, SEO specialists, accessibility auditors, and anyone who wants to understand what's really going on under the hood of a webpage.
